Privacy Policy

FamilyTime · Effective October 15, 2026

FamilyTime is a personal, non-commercial tool used by the adults in one family (parents and guardians) to keep track of school and parenting communication. This policy explains what data FamilyTime accesses, how it is used, and how to remove access. FamilyTime is not offered to the public, has no advertising, and does not sell or share data.

Google data FamilyTime accesses

PermissionWhat FamilyTime does with it
Read Gmail Finds parenting-related emails and notifications (from schools, teachers, coaches and parent groups) and reads their content to summarize them and find dates and action items. Other email is not read or stored.
Send Gmail Sends a daily summary email to the signed-in parent's own address. FamilyTime does not send email to anyone else.
Google Calendar Creates one dedicated calendar for the family's school events and adds, updates and removes events on that calendar only. Other calendars are not read or changed.

How data is processed and stored

  • Message content, summaries and event details are stored on the family's own computer.
  • To summarize messages, the text of relevant emails is sent to the Anthropic API (the Claude AI model) under Anthropic's commercial terms. Nothing else is sent to third parties.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the computer's secure system keychain, not in plain files.
  • Login links and access tokens found inside messages are removed before anything is stored.

Children's information

FamilyTime is used only by adults. School messages naturally mention the family's children (for example their class or team), and that information is used only to tell the parents which child a message concerns. FamilyTime does not collect information from children and is not directed to children.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than helping the family keep track of school and parenting communication. Data is not used to train AI models by FamilyTime.

Google API Services User Data Policy

FamilyTime's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • Stored data stays on the family's computer until it is deleted there; deleting FamilyTime's data folder removes it completely.
  • Any family member can revoke FamilyTime's access to their Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The dedicated calendar can be deleted from Google Calendar.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
