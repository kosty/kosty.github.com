FamilyTime

A private assistant that keeps a family on top of school and parenting messages.

Schools, coaches and parent groups send a steady stream of emails and notifications. FamilyTime reads those messages for the parents in one family and turns them into something easier to act on.

What it does

  • Reads parenting-related emails and notifications in the parent's own Google account.
  • Summarizes each message and notes which child it concerns.
  • Adds important dates to a dedicated calendar that FamilyTime creates.
  • Sends the parent a daily summary email with upcoming events and things that need action.

Who it is for

FamilyTime is a personal, non-commercial tool. It is used only by adults in one family: the parents and guardians who sign in with their own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy

Data stays with the family. Read the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed, where it is stored and how to revoke access.
